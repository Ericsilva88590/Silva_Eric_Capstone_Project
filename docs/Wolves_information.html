Wolf species

Red wolves

Red Wolves were originally found in western Louisiana east Texas however the red wolf population has decreased dramatically. The red wolves have been bought to North Carolina to repopulate and be a preserved species. There are about 20 red wolves that exist in an experimental population in North Carolina. Some ways that would help the red wolves survive would be road warning signs of red wolves, bright orange collars to be visible at night and also no hunting because red wolf killing is illegal. Most red wolves are killed by driving cars and the population keeps decreasing even though they were moved to North Carolina. The Red Wolves are smaller than a normal wolf that look more like a coyote and are the most endangered wolf species in the world. They bond in small packs of about 4 to 6 wolves and mainly eat small mammals like rabbits, rodents, and deer However the Red Wolf is very endangered that we are now taking care of them personally in order for them to survive and not go extinct.

Grey wolves

Grey Wolves are large wolves in the wild that are across the northern hemisphere and are the largest population of breed wolf types. Grey wolf packs vary from two to over thirty pack members. Ranging from 60 to 100 pounds female and 70 to 145 pounds male wolves weight. These wolves are made to be in the arctic cold surviving in the north with their fur being dense, thick underfur insulation against harsh climates. These wolves hunt large animals depending on the pack size, these animals are elk, moose, deer, bison and caribou. Can consume 20 pounds of meat in a single meal from a successful hunt but may go several days without eating prey if not available. Grey wolves often breed and when they do their pups become lone wolves seeking for another pack or a new pack. Even though these wolves are thriving in the northern hemisphere they are endangered animals due to habitat destruction in the U.S. In Alaska and Canada the wolves still roam but are instead being hunted by humans since they thrive more in the north. Colorado has reintroduced them to Yellowstone Park in 2023 to help restore natural ecosystem functions.

Other wolves

Other wolves will genre from Mexican wolves to the Vancouver Coastal Sea wolf. The Vancouver Sea wolf is on Vancouver Island and are very unique wolves because they have an aquatic lifestyle, meaning they live practically next to the ocean. These wolves hunt marine-based animals like fish and are known as mythical creatures for the people's spiritual beliefs. These wolves are unique in their swimming and have swimming capabilities unlike normal wolves. Hunting fish, salmon, mussels, and crabs, they have their unique ways of securing their food. Just like digging in the sand with their paws to find the animal or break open the shell with their jaws. Hunting is their main problem including the Takaya Lone Wolf which was shot and killed in 2020 by a hunter and is heavily documented. The Mexican wolf is a “lobo” since they live native to eastern and southeastern New Mexico, Arizona. Humans have dominated these wolves hunting them and destroying their habitat now the last Mexican wolves are being safely caught to not become extinct. In 2025 there are around 286 wild Mexican wolves and 45 in Mexico with 390 in captive breeding programs. These wolves live only from 6 to 8 years communicating with each other to hunt down their food. They will eat primarily large animals like elk, cows, and sometimes smaller animals like rabbits and squirrels. The reason the people killed the Mexican wolves were because the Mexican wolves were killing the livestock of the farmers therefore the wolves were taken away to survive from getting extinct.
